## One important correction/clarification

Your professor's Linux-router numerical has:

$$
\frac{40\text{ Bytes}}{2\text{ Gbps}}\approx160\text{ ns}
$$

Strictly speaking, if we convert **40 bytes = 320 bits**, then:

$$
\frac{320}{2\times10^9}
=160\text{ ns}
$$

So that calculation is correct.

Then:

$$
T_{\text{CPU}}=\frac{200}{2\times10^9}
=100\text{ ns}
$$

and because the packet is transferred **NIC → memory** and then **memory → NIC**:

$$
T_{\text{total}}
=160+100+160
=420\text{ ns}
$$

The resulting throughput is:

$$
\frac{320\text{ bits}}{420\text{ ns}}
\approx 761.9\text{ Mbps}
$$

So the lecture's approximately **760 Mbps** is correct.

This numerical is particularly important because it can easily be modified into an exam question.
